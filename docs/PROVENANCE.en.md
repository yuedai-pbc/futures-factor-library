# Provenance and implementation status

Bilingual edition: 2026-09-08.

30 research entries and 12 academic/method references. Signal definitions, position-management rules and PnL operators are distinct; they are not 30 independent alphas.

Canonical IDs, reference IDs, catalog counts, formulas and implementation evidence are preserved across languages. English text is an editorial translation of the research descriptions, not a new claim of empirical validity. Source identifiers may contain Chinese characters by design.

- The catalog includes risk measurement, sizing and accounting, not just predictive signals.
- No authorized market-data backtest has been completed; basic trend, basis and contract-PnL examples are available.
- Inventory and commercial-position features apply only to appropriate commodities, not mechanically to equity-index, rate or FX futures.
- Log basis is undefined for non-positive prices; use a pre-registered alternative rather than silently taking absolute values.

The repository includes curated definitions, a static website and independent basic examples. It does not redistribute complete papers, confidential accounts, raw proprietary market data or unverified third-party source code. Original literature results are not local backtest performance.
